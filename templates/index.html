🌿 5-4-3-2-1 Grounding Technique

This technique anchors you to the present moment and stops a panic spiral. Do this slowly:

5
Things you can SEE
Look around and name 5 things visible to you right now.
4
Things you can TOUCH
Notice 4 textures or sensations around you.
3
Things you can HEAR
Tune into 3 sounds in your environment.
2
Things you can SMELL
Identify 2 scents, or recall a calming scent.
1
Thing you can TASTE
Notice 1 taste currently in your mouth.
